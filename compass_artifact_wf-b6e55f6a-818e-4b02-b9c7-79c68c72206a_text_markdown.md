# Singapore Independent Compliance Consultant Prospect List for ComplianceAssist

## TL;DR
- **The 15 prospects below — concentrated in roughly seven boutique firms (Integrity Consulting, Ingenia Consultants/Alpadis, Alpha Compliance, HM Strategy, Ethikom, Cambridge Advisers/CAGlobe, Singapore Compliance) plus two solo / ex-large-consultancy operators — are the most actionable independent MAS-compliance specialists for ComplianceAssist to engage as advisors, channel partners and design-partner customers.** Together they cover MAS CMS licensing, LFMC/RFMC/VCFM, family offices, AML/CFT, payment services, digital assets, and internal audit, and several are explicit RegTech advocates.
- **Start with two warm-call clusters:** (a) RegTech-friendly boutiques (Ethikom, Cambridge Advisers, Singapore Compliance, Ingenia/Fadzlun) who already evaluate RegTech for their FMC clients, and (b) ex-regulator solo/principal voices (Sinyee Koh, Lay Geok Yeo, Nizam Ismail) who shape buyer opinion via AIMA, IMAS, IBF and the Singapore FinTech Association.
- **Treat large consolidators (Waystone, IQ-EQ, Ocorian/Bovill Newgate) as out-of-scope per the brief**, but consider individual practice leads inside them — particularly Billie-Jo Dixon (Ocorian Singapore) and Sachin Gandhi (formerly Argus Global) — as bridge contacts who can still influence smaller boutique networks.

## Key Findings

The Singapore independent MAS-compliance market has consolidated sharply in the last 48 months: Argus Global merged into Waystone Compliance Solutions on 16 June 2021 (per Waystone's press release: *"LONDON, 16 June 2021 — Waystone…has today announced the creation of a new global compliance offering via the merging of four compliance service providers [including] Argus Global"*); ComplianceAsia and Lymon Pte Ltd were acquired by IQ-EQ on 27 June 2023 (*"IQ-EQ…today announced the acquisition of Lymon and ComplianceAsia, two transactions that make IQ-EQ the market leading independent Regulatory Compliance business globally"*); Ocorian acquired Bovill in February 2024 and combined it with its pre-existing Newgate Compliance subsidiary, launching as "Bovill Newgate" in June 2024 and rebranding into Ocorian on 31 July 2025; and Ingenia Consultants was acquired 100% by Alpadis on 13 August 2025 via an all-share exchange.

Despite this, a tight cluster of genuinely independent boutiques (≤20 staff) still serves the long tail of LFMCs, RFMCs, VCFMs, family offices and fintechs — exactly ComplianceAssist's ICP. The strongest prospects are former MAS regulators or ex-private-bank/ex-Big-4 compliance heads who set up their own practices since 2015-2018, and are visibly active in industry forums (AIMA Singapore Regulatory Committee, IMAS, the Hubbis Compliance in Asian Wealth Management Forum, IBF, Singapore FinTech Association).

## Caveats

- **Consolidation risk:** Four of the historically strongest Singapore boutiques (Argus, ComplianceAsia, Bovill, Ingenia) have all been acquired by larger global groups between June 2021 and August 2025. Their founder-principals (Gandhi, Allen, Dixon, Haudenschild) may be subject to non-competes or earn-out incentives that constrain reseller deals. Confirm contractual freedom before pitching reseller economics.
- **Small ICP scale:** Most of these boutiques run 2–20-person teams. Per-seat SaaS deals will be modest; the real economic value is using them as channel partners into their 30–100 FMC client books.
- **Email-address confidence:** sinyee.koh@integrity-consult.com and yiphan.kew@ingenia-consultants.com are published on the firms' own websites and confirmed. All other email addresses (compliance.com.sg team, Alpha Compliance team, Ethikom) should be inferred from firm-domain patterns and verified via the firms' contact forms or via LinkedIn InMail before bulk outreach.
- **LinkedIn URLs:** Verified URLs for Sinyee Koh, Smitha Prabhu, Sachin Gandhi, Nizam Ismail and Nicole Chen are confirmed from public profile pages. For the Ingenia team (Phoebe Mok, Kew Yip Han, Victor Chan, Fadzlun), the firm's LinkedIn page (https://sg.linkedin.com/company/ingenia-consultants) is the entry point — individual URLs vary by their privacy settings.
